
It is an interactive Rock, Paper, Scissors game built in Python. The player competes against a randomised computer opponent across unlimited rounds, with scores tracked for wins, losses, and ties until the player chooses to quit.

The player is repeatedly prompted to enter a move (rock, paper, or scissors) or "Q" to quit.

Invalid inputs are ignored, and the player is re-prompted without ending the game.

The computer's move is randomly selected using the random module.

Conditional logic compares the player's move against the computer's move to determine the outcome of each round (win, lose, or tie).

Running totals for wins, losses, and ties are tracked across the session and displayed when the game ends.
